Bulk Email
Bulk email to your subscriber list — Amazon SES, suppression-aware, one-click unsubscribe. Subscribers here are separate from your CRM contacts.

Bulk Email — Help

What this is

A dedicated engine for sending bulk email to your subscriber list through Amazon SES. It is separate from your CRM contacts and from one-off emails you send from a contact record — this is for list-wide campaigns and newsletters.

1 · Build your lists

On the Subscribers tab, import addresses (paste or CSV) and file them into lists — for example Restaurants and Brokers. One database, multiple lists; you target the right list when you send. Every list shares one suppression list, so an unsubscribe covers all of them.

2 · Compose the email

Create a campaign, pick a template style, and edit in the visual block editor — header, hero, text, buttons, stats, signature, footer. Pull from a listing to auto-fill a campaign from one of your live listings. Always send yourself a test before a real blast.

3 · Choose the audience

Set the campaign to send to a whole list or a filtered segment, so the right subscribers get the right message. The recipient count updates as you narrow it.

4 · Schedule the send

Send now, or schedule one or more times. Repeats let the same blast go out on a cadence. Auto-scheduling stays on business days — never weekends or major holidays, and one send per morning / one per afternoon system-wide. For an intentional holiday send (a Christmas greeting), tick Deliberate send to lift the blackout for that time.

5 · Track results

The Campaigns tab lists every blast with opens, clicks, unsubscribes, and bounces. Scheduled sends also appear on your system calendar.

Suppression

Anyone on the Suppression tab is permanently excluded from every send, tenant-wide. Bounces, complaints, and one-click unsubscribes land here automatically; you can add addresses by hand when someone asks off another way.

Deliverability

Keep lists clean and only mail people who opted in. High bounce or complaint rates hurt your sending reputation — the suppression list and one-click unsubscribe protect it for you.

Bulk Email — Features

What's inside

  • Amazon SES sending — throttled, high-deliverability bulk delivery.
  • Subscriber lists & segments — one database, many lists (Restaurants, Brokers, …); target by list or filter.
  • Import — paste or CSV upload straight into a chosen list.
  • Visual template editor — drag-free block editor: header, hero, text, stats, buttons, signature, footer.
  • Brokerage-branded styles — templates pull your logo and colors from Admin; three selectable looks.
  • Pull from a listing — auto-fill a campaign from one of your live listings.
  • Test sends — fire a proof to yourself before any blast.
  • Robust scheduling — send now, or one or many scheduled times.
  • Repeat blasts — run the same campaign on a cadence.
  • Weekend & holiday blackout — auto-scheduling stays on business days; one AM + one PM per day.
  • Deliberate holiday send — override the blackout for an intentional greeting.
  • Calendar integration — scheduled blasts show on your system calendar.
  • A/B testing — split subject lines and let the winner send to the rest.
  • Open / click / bounce tracking — full per-campaign metrics.
  • Shared suppression list — bounces, complaints, and one-click unsubscribes auto-excluded tenant-wide.
  • Up to 50 concurrent campaigns — managed in one list view with search and archive.
