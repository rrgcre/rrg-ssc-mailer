How a deal moves through the toolkitThe seller side, front to back — each step hands off to the next automatically.
1

Qualification Call

Log a prospective-seller call and read their real motivation. Advance the good ones.

2

Seller Interview

The deep listing profile — operations, lease, add-backs. Feeds the valuation.

3

Business Valuation

The analyst drafts a defensible BOV. Review, edit, then advance it.

4

Marketing Pack

The CIM, email templates, and a lease abstract — all from the locked numbers.

5

Data Room & Attack Plan

A confidential buyer room and the go-to-market strategy for the deal.

6

Offers → Close

Track offers, tours & NDAs on the deal file, then run the Commission Forecaster.

You rarely start from scratch — advancing one step carries everything forward, so the numbers and the story stay consistent end to end. The whole chain lives on one deal file under Deals.
The CRM behind itCompanies, Contacts & Deals — your whole book of relationships.
Every deal ties to a Company (the business being sold) and its Contacts (owners, partners, buyers). Open a contact to see everything they touch across your book — their company, deals, and every offer, tour, and NDA in their name. Companies and contacts are created automatically as you work deals, so the CRM fills itself.
