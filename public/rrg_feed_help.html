Activity Feed — Help & User Guide

The Feed is your command center — everything happening across your book in one stream, newest first, with the two things a broker actually opens it for pulled to the front: who's engaging in your confidential data rooms, and what needs you today. This guide covers every part of it.

1 Overview

The Feed answers three questions the moment you open it: what moved, what needs me, and what's going quiet. The center column is the live stream of activity; the right rail is your ranked to-do list.

The core idea

  • One place for everything — calls, tours, notes, emails, meetings, agreements, and data-room engagement, all on the same timeline.
  • The money signal up front — when a buyer opens the P&L, the lease, or the CIM in a data room, it lands in the feed and is flagged hot.
  • Triage, not just reporting — the Keep things moving rail ranks what needs you: overdue tasks, hot buyers, and deals going quiet — each with a one-click action.
  • Signal over noise — automated workflow churn is folded away under one toggle so real activity stands out.
Where to find it
Open Feed from the left nav. The What your feed does strip at the top of that page links to the matching section of this guide — tap any tile.

2 Your whole book, one stream

The center column is every logged activity across all your contacts, newest first — so nothing lives in one person's head or a separate app.

What shows up here

  • Contact activity — calls, tours, meals, texts, emails, notes and to-dos logged on any contact.
  • Deal milestones — agreements sent and signed, LOIs sent, received and countered, new contacts added.
  • Data-room engagement — buyer visits to your confidential rooms (see the next section).
  • Calendar meetings — tours, calls and meetings from your calendar (section 5).

Click any row that's tied to a contact to jump straight to that contact's page. Rows are colored by type with a dot on the left so you can scan the stream at a glance.

3 Who's in your data rooms

The single most valuable signal in the whole system. When a buyer opens documents in one of your confidential data rooms, it shows up right here — so you know where the interest is before you make your next call.

How to read a data-room row

  • Who and where — the buyer's name and the business/data room they were in.
  • What they opened — how many documents, and specifically if they touched the money: P&L, tax returns, the lease, rent roll, SDE/EBITDA, the CIM or offering.
  • Downloads — how many documents they pulled, not just viewed.
The tell
A buyer in the financials or the lease this week is flagged ● Hot and the row is tinted. That's the closest thing to reading a buyer's mind — call them while the interest is warm.

A buyer's repeated hits in one sitting are grouped into a single visit so the feed reads cleanly instead of one row per click. Click any data-room row to open the room and see the full engagement breakdown.

5 Meetings on the timeline

Your calendar and your activity are the same story, so meetings sit right on the feed alongside everything else.

  • Recent and upcoming — meetings, tours and calls from roughly the last two months through the next few weeks.
  • Upcoming flag — anything still in the future is tagged Upcoming so you can tell a booked meeting from a completed one.
  • Tied to the contact — a meeting linked to a contact clicks straight through to that contact's page.
Related
Book and manage meetings on the Calendar — everything you schedule there appears here automatically.

6 Signal over noise

A feed is only useful if the important things aren't buried. Automated, low-value activity is treated as system activity and folded out of the way by default.

How it works

  • Hidden by default — the System activity toggle starts Off, so you see real, human and deal activity first.
  • Folded, not lost — repeated automated updates on the same contact (like screening skips and undos, or enrichment runs) collapse into a single · +N more row.
  • One click to see it all — flip System activity: On any time to expand the full audit trail.
Why
The board's plumbing generates a lot of housekeeping. It belongs in the record, not in your face — so it's one toggle away instead of the first thing you scroll past.

7 Saved views

Set the filters you use most, then save them so you can jump back in one click.

  1. Set your scope (everyone or just you), activity type, user, and the system toggle the way you want them.
  2. Click ＋ Save this view in the left rail and give it a name.
  3. The view appears in the rail — click it any time to snap the feed back to exactly those filters.

Views are remembered on this browser. Delete one with the × beside it.

8 Filters

Slice the stream any way you work.

  • All activity / My activity — everything across the team, or just what you did (admins and owners).
  • By user — narrow to a single teammate's activity.
  • By type — show only calls, only agreements, only data-room engagement, and so on.
  • System activity On / Off — show or hide the automated audit trail (section 6).

9 People worth adding

The right rail surfaces people you email often but haven't saved as contacts yet — pulled from your inbox.

  • From your inbox — frequent correspondents from the last couple of months who aren't in your book.
  • One-click add — hit Add to create the contact without leaving the feed.
  • Automated senders filtered out — no-reply and notification addresses are skipped.
Requires
Gmail connected on your Account page.

10 FAQ & troubleshooting

A buyer opened documents but I don't see it.

Data-room engagement only appears once a buyer with a personal access grant opens a document. Owner previews of your own room aren't counted. Give it a moment after their visit and refresh.

Why did the skip / undo notes disappear?

They didn't — they're system activity now. Flip System activity: On to see them, and repeated ones are folded into a single row.

I don't see any email nudges or suggested people.

Those come from Gmail. Connect it on your Account page and they'll populate.

What makes a buyer "hot"?

They opened a deal-critical document — the P&L, tax returns, the lease, rent roll, SDE/EBITDA, or the CIM — within the last week. That's the money signal.

Can I see just my own activity?

Yes — click My activity (admins and owners). Reps see their own book by default.
