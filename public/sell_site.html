How it works

You stay in control at every step. Nothing goes to a buyer until you say so.

  1. See your range

    Two minutes of questions about sales, costs, rent and how many locations you run. The range reflects what buyers pay for Texas restaurants and groups with similar earnings.

  2. Talk it through

    A specialist calls within one business day, goes through your numbers location by location, and tells you what a sale of the group, or part of it, would look like.

  3. Meet the buyers

    If you decide to sell, we market your group without naming it, screen every buyer for experience and funds, and run the deal through closing, including landlord consents on every lease.

Questions owners ask

Are you buying my restaurant?

No. We represent restaurant owners and bring the buyers to you: operators, multi-unit groups and investors. That way you get more than one offer, not just ours.

Will my staff or customers find out?

Not from us. Listings never show the name or address, and every buyer signs an NDA before they learn which restaurant it is.

Can I sell some locations and keep the rest?

Yes. Many buyers want a few units in one market, and selling weaker locations can make the rest of the group stronger. We value each location and the group as a whole so you can compare.

How accurate is the range?

It’s a starting point built from the numbers you enter. Your lease, equipment, trend and books all move the price. We confirm a number once we’ve seen your financials.

What does it cost?

The value range and the first conversation are free. If you sell through us, a success fee is paid at closing. Nothing is owed if you don’t sell.
