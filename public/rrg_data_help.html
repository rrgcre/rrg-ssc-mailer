Data — Help & User Guide

Everything you do in the Data section, in the order a new user should do it: bring your book in from your old CRM, clean it up, remove duplicates, link contacts to their companies, then enrich it with live Google data, logos, and concept detail. By the end your list of names becomes a real restaurant & bar database you can work from.

1 Overview

The Data tools live under Settings → Data. They take you from a raw CRM export to a clean, enriched book of business — companies, their locations, the contacts at each one, and the concepts (restaurant/bar brands) they operate.

The golden path

1Import→ 2Clean up→ 3Duplicates→ 4Link & enrich→ ↻Clean up again

This is the same recommended order printed across the top of the Data Enrichment screen. Import first, tidy what came in, collapse duplicates, then link and enrich — and re-run cleanup at the end to standardize anything the enrichment added.

Two kinds of tools

It helps to know which tools cost nothing and which use AI:

  • Deterministic Cleanup, Find Duplicates, Match by Email Domain — rules-based, instant, no AI cost.
  • External data Enrich from Google — pulls live business data from Google (uses your Google Maps key, not AI).
  • AI Find missing logos (AI) and Concept Intelligence — use your Anthropic key and are metered on the AI Usage page.
Who this is for
Admins setting up the firm's book for the first time, and anyone maintaining data quality as new records come in. Most tools are review-and-approve — you tick what you want before it's applied. The one exception is Cleanup & Standardize, which runs and applies its fixes automatically, since they're deterministic and safe.

2 Quick start — your first hour

Brand new and staring at an empty system? Do these seven things in order and you'll go from a spreadsheet to a working database.

  1. Import your contacts and companies from your old CRM. Export a spreadsheet from Copper, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive — or any CSV/Excel — and drop it in. Step 1.
  2. Clean up & standardize what came in — fix ALL-CAPS names, state codes, phone formats, lowercased emails, and let it set the main contact where a company has only one. Step 2.
  3. Find duplicates and merge the obvious ones so you're not working the same company twice. Step 3.
  4. Match by email domain to link loose contacts to the right company automatically. Step 4.
  5. Enrich from Google to pull operating status, ratings, price point, phone, website, and map location onto every location. Step 5.
  6. Find logos so companies show a real brand mark instead of a grey initial. Step 6.
  7. Run Concept Intelligence to fill in cuisine, concept type, and price point and flag the multi-unit operators. Step 7.
Don't aim for perfect on day one
Import and a single cleanup pass already make the system usable. Enrichment, logos, and concept detail can run in the background afterward — each is review-and-approve and safe to do in chunks.

3 Step 1 · Import your data

Bring your companies and contacts in from any CRM in three steps. Open Settings → Data → Import Data.

What you can import

Three record types, each its own import: Companies, Contacts, and Tasks. Works with exports from Copper, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, or any plain CSV / Excel file.

  1. Pick what you're importing — Companies, Contacts, or Tasks — then upload your .xlsx or .csv export.
  2. Map the columns — the importer auto-detects your columns (name, email, phone, company, address, and so on). Check the mapping and adjust anything it guessed wrong. At minimum, map the name column.
  3. Preview & import — see a sample of what will come in, then import. As it runs it dedupes and links every contact to its company automatically.

Order matters a little

If you're importing both, do Companies first, then Contacts — that way each contact can attach to a company that already exists. If you only have one combined contact list, that's fine too; the importer creates the companies it needs from the company column.

Every import is reversible

Each import is saved as a numbered batch on the same page under past imports, showing what it added, by whom, and when. If a file came in wrong, hit Revert Import on that batch to remove exactly the records it added — no manual cleanup.

Revert is permanent
Reverting deletes the records that batch created and can't be undone. It only touches that batch, so it's safe — but double-check you're reverting the right one.

4 Step 2 · Clean up & standardize Deterministic

Fix the messy stuff imports leave behind. Open Settings → Data → Data Enrichment → Cleanup & Standardize. No AI, no cost — just rules.

What it proposes

FixWhat it does
Name casingALL-CAPS or lowercase names title-cased (e.g. JOE'S BAR → Joe's Bar).
State codesState names to two-letter codes (Texas → TX).
WebsitesNormalized to a clean https://domain form.
EmailsLowercased.
Phone formatStandardized to (555) 555-5555, on companies and locations.
Market from cityAssigns a metro/market when it's blank but the city is known.
Decision-maker tagsTags a contact Decision Maker when their title implies it (owner, partner, GM…).
Sole contact as mainWhen a company has exactly one contact, sets that contact as its main contact.

How to run it

Open the tool and it just runs — it scans your book and applies every fix automatically, no ticking or approval. When it's done it shows a summary and lists exactly what it changed, grouped by record, so you can see what happened. A Run again button re-runs it any time. Because the fixes are deterministic and idempotent, re-running is safe — it only ever touches records that still need standardizing.

Set the main contact once, forever
The Sole contact as main fix backfills your imported book in one pass. Going forward, whenever you add a contact to a company that has no main contact yet, the app makes that contact the main automatically — so it stays correct without re-running cleanup.

5 Step 3 · Find duplicates Deterministic

Imports from more than one source almost always create doubles. Open Find Duplicates from the Data Enrichment screen.

  1. Scan — the tool groups likely-duplicate companies (or contacts) together. On a large book this takes a moment; you can keep it running or cancel.
  2. Review each group — see the records that look like the same company side by side.
  3. Merge — merging keeps one record and folds the others into it, preserving their contacts, locations, deals, and the main contact, so nothing is lost.
Merge is a combine, not a delete
The kept record inherits the others' details; the duplicates are absorbed. Review the group before merging so you keep the record you want as the survivor.

6 Step 4 · Link by email domain Deterministic

Contacts often import without being attached to a company. Match by Email Domain links them the reliable way — by the domain in their email, never by name.

  • A contact at jane@magnoliahaus.com links to the company that owns magnoliahaus.com.
  • Personal domains are ignored — gmail, yahoo, outlook and the like never create a false match.
  • Review the proposed links, tick the ones you want, and apply. Each becomes a chip you can see before committing.
Why domain, not name
Two different companies can share a name; an email domain is unique to one business. Matching on the domain is why this step is safe to trust.

7 Step 5 · Enrich from Google External data

Turn each location into a live record. Enrich from Google reads Google for every location and pulls back the operating detail you'd otherwise chase down by hand.

What it pulls

Operating status (open / dark / closed), star rating and review count, price point, phone, website, and map coordinates — live onto every location. When you apply, it also pulls location photos from Google for any location that doesn't have any yet, so your locations come back with pictures, not just data.

How to run it

  1. The screen shows how many locations haven't been pulled yet. Click to scan.
  2. A progress bar shows how far along it is and an estimated time remaining; when it finishes you get a summary of how many matched Google and how many had no match.
  3. Review the suggested changes per location and apply the ones you want. After the data applies, it pulls photos for the enriched companies — a second progress bar shows that pass, and you can stop it any time.
Needs a Google key
This tool needs a Google Maps key set under Settings → Integrations. Until then the tool shows "add a key." It uses Google, not AI, so there's no Anthropic cost.

8 Step 6 · Find logos

Give every company a real brand mark. Find Logos pulls a logo for each company that has a website, and can use AI to track down the ones that don't.

Two passes

  • From the website External — for companies with a website, it fetches the best available logo. Companies with a real logo sort to the top and start pre-approved; favicon-only guesses start unchecked so you approve just the good ones.
  • Find missing logos (AI) AI — for companies with no website, AI figures out the brand and finds a logo. Newly found logos sort to the top of the grid so they're easy to review and approve first.

Approve the ones that look right and Apply. Applied logos show up on the company everywhere in the app.

AI note
The "Find missing logos (AI)" pass uses your Anthropic key and is metered on AI Usage. The website pass is free. A working dialog with a live progress estimate shows while either runs.

9 Step 7 · Concept Intelligence AI

The step that makes this a restaurant & bar database, not just a contact list. Concept Intelligence reads each concept and fills the fields a plain import leaves blank.

What it fills

  • Cuisine — the food category, from your firm's cuisine list.
  • Concept type — full-service, fast-casual, QSR, bar / nightlife, café / bakery, and so on.
  • Price point — $ through $$$$.
  • Multi-unit operators — flags companies that run more than one concept, so you can spot the groups.

Press Classify with AI, watch the progress and estimate, then review the suggestions and apply the ones you want. It only fills fields that are blank — it won't overwrite what you've already set. Applied concepts clear out of the review grid so you can see what's left.

AI note
Uses your Anthropic key and is metered on AI Usage. Runs in review-and-approve chunks so a large book is safe to classify.

10 The loop & ongoing upkeep

Enrichment can introduce new websites, phones, and details — so the recommended workflow ends where it began: clean up again.

  • Re-run Cleanup after a big enrichment pass to standardize anything new (phone formats, website forms, markets from newly-found cities).
  • The Data Enrichment home reads the state of your book — each tool card shows how much is left to do (companies missing a logo, locations not yet pulled from Google, concepts missing detail), so you always know what to run next.
  • New records self-maintain where they can — a sole contact becomes the main contact on add; imports dedupe and link as they land.

11 Record counts — know your book

Settings → Data → Record Counts is the one-glance tally of everything in the system: contacts, companies, listings, data rooms, concepts, locations, files, valuations, marketing packs, LOIs, agreements, interviews, and screenings.

Use it as a health check before and after a big import or enrichment run — the numbers should move the way you expect. There's a Refresh to re-tally on demand.

12 Export & backup

Export your data

Settings → Data → Export Data pulls your companies and contacts back out to a spreadsheet whenever you need a copy outside the system.

Data backup

Settings → Data → Data Backup keeps a full copy of everything the team has built — deals, companies, contacts, requests, calls, valuations, marketing packs, and every data-room file. The server saves an automatic snapshot once a day; download one any time to keep an off-site copy in Drive or Dropbox, or restore from a backup file if you ever need to roll back.

Before any big change
Grab a fresh backup before a large import, merge, or restore. It's the safety net that lets you move fast on the rest.

13 FAQ & troubleshooting

Where do I start as a brand-new user?

Import Data first, then run Cleanup & Standardize. Those two make the system usable. Duplicates, email linking, Google enrichment, logos, and Concept Intelligence build on top — do them in that order when you're ready.

Will these tools overwrite what I've already entered?

Enrichment, duplicates, email-linking, logos, and Concept Intelligence are review-and-approve — they propose changes and nothing is written until you apply, and the AI steps only fill blank fields. Cleanup & Standardize is the exception: it runs and applies automatically, but it only standardizes formatting (casing, codes, phone/email formats) and sets a main contact where there's a single obvious one — it never discards information.

I imported the wrong file.

Open Import Data, find that numbered batch under past imports, and click Revert Import. It removes exactly the records that batch added.

Enrich from Google says "add a key."

Set a Google Maps key under Settings → Integrations. That tool reads Google, so it needs the key before it can pull anything.

Which tools cost money?

Find missing logos (AI) and Concept Intelligence use your Anthropic key and show on the AI Usage page. Cleanup, Duplicates, and Email-domain matching are free. Google enrichment uses your Google key, not AI.

How do I set a company's main contact?

If a company has one contact, Cleanup sets it as main automatically. For companies with several contacts, open the company and choose the main contact yourself — that's a judgment call the system won't guess.

How long does enrichment take?

It runs in batches and shows a live time estimate. A large book can take several minutes; you can let it run or cancel and pick it up later — it remembers what's already done.

14 Glossary

CompanyAn operator or business entity — the parent record that holds locations, concepts, and contacts.
ConceptA restaurant or bar brand a company operates. One company can run several concepts.
LocationA physical address for a concept — what Google enrichment reads against.
ContactA person at a company. The main contact is the primary person for that company.
StandardizeRules-based cleanup — casing, codes, formats — with no AI cost.
EnrichAdd data the record didn't have: Google business detail, a logo, cuisine / type / price.
Multi-unit operatorA company that runs more than one concept — flagged by Concept Intelligence.
Import batchOne numbered import, revertible as a unit.
