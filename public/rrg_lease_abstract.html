Lease Abstract — Help

What this is

A concise summary of a lease’s key business terms — parties, premises, term, rent, options, charges, and risk items — for fast review and buyer packages. It never replaces the executed lease.

Occupancy & economics

The Occupancy & Economics section turns the lease into the numbers a buyer’s lender and your BOV care about. Enter annual base rent, NNN/CAM + taxes + insurance, and total occupancy cost, plus the business’s annual sales (from the P&L or BOV), and it computes occupancy cost as a % of sales live — green under 10%, amber 10–15%, red above — the single best read on whether the rent is healthy for a restaurant. Capture key money / lease value and a financeability note (does the remaining term, with options, support SBA financing?) so the lease-exit value is front and center. These figures also flow into the CIM’s Occupancy & Lease Summary.

Edit in place

With Editing: On, click any value and type. Changes save automatically a moment after you stop typing — the status reads “Unsaved changes…” then “Saved ✓” with the time. You can also click Save any time to write immediately. Turn editing off for a clean, read-only view.

Add rows

In edit mode, use + Add under Watch Items, Base Rent, and Critical Dates to add lines.

Redaction

Redact Landlord and Redact Tenant withhold those party names, replacing them with “Withheld — available under NDA.” Use this for buyer-facing copies before an NDA is signed; the guarantor follows the tenant.

Print / PDF

Print opens your browser’s print dialog — choose “Save as PDF” for a clean file. The toolbar and edit highlights are hidden automatically.

Confidential

Every abstract carries a confidentiality notice and disclaimer in the footer. Treat it as confidential work product.
