Calendar — Help & User Guide

The Calendar is where every meeting in the shop lives in one place — listing walk-throughs, buyer calls, seller tours, and follow-ups — whether you booked it here or from a contact's page. Schedule with a contact in your book or anyone outside the group, invite guests by email in a click, and see the whole team's day at a glance. This guide covers every part of it.

1 Overview

The Calendar is a shared, team-wide schedule of meetings. Anything you book — from the Calendar page or from a contact's Calendar card — shows up here, so nothing lives in one person's head or a separate app.

The core idea

  • One place for every meeting — reps and admins book here or from a contact record, and it all rolls into the same calendar.
  • Tied to your book of business — link a meeting to a contact and it appears on that contact's timeline too, so the schedule and the relationship stay connected.
  • Guests don't need the app — attach anyone's name and email and send them a real calendar invite; they add it to whatever calendar they already use.
  • Team visibility — admins can see everyone's calendar or filter to one rep; reps see their own.
Where to find it
Open Calendar from the left nav. The What your calendar can do strip at the top of that page links to the matching section of this guide — tap any tile.

2 Scheduling a meeting

Two ways to start a meeting, whichever is faster for the moment you're in.

A. The "+ New meeting" button

On the Calendar page, click + New meeting (top-right of the toolbar) to open a blank meeting form.

B. Click a day

In Month or Week view, click any day cell and the form opens pre-set to that date — handy when you're eyeing an open slot.

C. From a contact's page

On any contact, use the + on the Calendar card to book a meeting already tied to that person. It lands on the shared calendar automatically — see Staying in sync.

  1. Title — what the meeting is (e.g. "Listing walk-through"). Required.
  2. Contact — optional; start typing to match a contact in your book, or leave blank for an internal or outside meeting.
  3. Type, Date, Time — the meeting type comes from your firm's list; pick a date and a start time. A date is required.
  4. End & Location — an end time and where it happens: a street address, a video link, or a phone number.
  5. Attendees & Notes — add outside guests by name and email, and any prep or agenda notes.
  6. Save — the meeting posts to the calendar. Optionally email invites at the same time (next section).
Best practice
Link the contact whenever the meeting is with someone in your book. That's what puts the meeting on their record and keeps your follow-up history in one place.

3 Day, Week, Month & Agenda

Four views of the same schedule. Switch with the segmented control in the toolbar; move through time with ‹, Today, and ›.

ViewBest for
MonthThe big picture — every day of the month, up to four meetings per day with a "+N more" when a day is busy.
WeekSeven columns side by side — a working week at a glance.
DayA single day's agenda in order, with times and details.
AgendaA running list of what's coming up, grouped by day — your upcoming schedule top to bottom.

Click any meeting in any view to open it and edit. Each teammate's meetings show in their own color, so on a shared view you can tell whose is whose at a glance — the color key is in the left rail, and your own row is marked (you). Tasks show in amber.

4 Attendees & calendar invites

Add guests who aren't on the platform and send them a real calendar invite — no account, no app.

  1. Add attendees — on the meeting form, enter each guest's name and email. Use + Add attendee for more than one.
  2. Email the invite — tick Email a calendar invite to attendees now before saving, and everyone with an email gets an invite they can add to their own calendar.
  3. Send later — on an existing meeting, Send invite re-sends to the attendees anytime after the fact.
Reading the form
The "Email a calendar invite" option only appears when the firm's email sending is set up. Attendees need an email address on file to receive an invite.
From a contact's page
When you schedule from a contact's Calendar card and Gmail is connected, saving can also hand off to Google Calendar with the meeting pre-filled and the contact added as a guest — so it lands on your Google calendar too.

5 Whose calendar you see

Control which meetings the calendar shows.

  • Admins — a picker switches between Everyone's calendar, Just my meetings, or any single rep's calendar, so you can see the whole team or zero in on one person.
  • Reps — see your own meetings, plus any teammate’s calendar that person has shared with you (use the My + shared calendars picker). Share your own from your Account page.
Note
Deleting a meeting is limited to the person who created it — you can open and read anyone's meeting (per your access), but only its owner can remove it.

6 Meeting details

Every meeting carries the context you'll want when you glance at it later.

  • Type — Meeting, Call, Tour, Follow-up, and any others your firm defines. Edit the list under Admin settings.
  • Location — a street address, a video link, or a phone number, whatever fits the meeting.
  • Notes — agenda, prep, or anything to remember, kept with the meeting.
  • Status — a cancelled meeting stays on the calendar shown struck through, so the record's honest without cluttering your day.

7 Staying in sync

The Calendar page and each contact's Calendar card are two windows on the same set of meetings.

  • Book once, see everywhere — a meeting scheduled from a contact's page appears on the shared calendar automatically, and a calendar meeting linked to a contact shows on that contact's record.
  • Edit anywhere — open a meeting from either place to change the time, details, or attendees.
  • Google Calendar hand-off — with Gmail connected, scheduling from a contact can also push the meeting to Google Calendar with the contact as a guest.
Why it matters
Because the contact card and the team calendar draw from the same meetings, your follow-up radar and your schedule never drift apart.

8 Google-powered features

Google Workspace calendar power, right inside RRG. Most of these are live now; two are still on the roadmap and are marked below. Each can be turned on or off for the whole team under Settings → Calendar.

All-in-one booking

Booking pages let clients, customers, and partners book time with you directly. Turn on your booking page under Account, set your meeting length, and share the link — clients pick an open slot from your working hours and it drops onto your calendar automatically, with no back-and-forth email.

Track tasks on your calendar

Add a date and time to any task and it appears on your calendar automatically, right alongside your meetings — so your to-dos and your schedule live in one view. Toggle them with the ✓ Tasks button on the Calendar toolbar.

Sync events from Gmail On the roadmap

Flights, hotels, and restaurant reservations sitting in your inbox will be added to your calendar automatically, so travel and bookings show up without you entering them by hand.

Collaborate with Google Meet

Open a meeting and click 🎥 Add Google Meet (when your Google account is connected) to mint a real Meet link on the event in one click, so you and your attendees can connect virtually without leaving the calendar.

Share calendars with others

Share your calendar with specific teammates from your Account page, and they can see your meetings on the Calendar — pick My + shared calendars or a person by name in the calendar picker. Admins already see everyone; this lets any rep open their calendar to the people they work closely with. (Sharing is view-only.)

Attach files to events

Consolidate the details in one place by attaching the relevant agendas, pre-reads, and documents directly to an event — everyone opens the meeting and finds exactly what they need.

Set working location and hours

Let your teammates know where you'll be working from and what your working hours are, so the team schedules around your real availability.

On the roadmap
Auto-syncing flights, hotels and reservations from Gmail is still in planning. Want to weigh in on what ships next? Add it to the Wish List.

9 FAQ & troubleshooting

I don't see the "Email a calendar invite" option.

That option only shows when the firm's email sending is configured. Once it's set up, the checkbox appears on the meeting form and the Send invite button appears on saved meetings.

My guest didn't get the invite.

Make sure the attendee has an email address on the meeting. Only attendees with an email receive an invite. You can re-send anytime with Send invite on the meeting.

Can I book a meeting with someone who isn't a contact?

Yes. Leave the Contact field blank and add the person as an attendee by name and email — good for internal meetings or one-off outside guests.

A meeting I booked on a contact's page isn't on the calendar.

It should appear automatically. Check the calendar's scope filter — if you're viewing "Just my meetings" or another rep, switch to Everyone's calendar (admins) or confirm the meeting is under your name.

How do I cancel without losing the record?

Open the meeting and mark it cancelled — it stays on the calendar struck through so the history is intact. Only the meeting's creator can delete it outright.

What do the meeting colors mean?

Each person has their own color, so meetings are colored by whoever owns them. The Calendars key in the left rail lists everyone currently in view with their color; your own is marked (you). Tasks are amber, and a struck-through title means the meeting was cancelled.
