Admin — Help & User Guide

The admin section — everything under Settings — is where you configure and run the platform: who's on the team and what they can do, how deals move, how your data stays clean, the paperwork you generate, your brand, your integrations, and the AI. This guide is a map of those controls.

Admins onlyConfigure the platformSettings

1 Overview

Open Settings from the Admin menu. The left rail groups every admin tool; the panel on the right is where you make changes. Most tools are self-explanatory; this guide explains what each area is for and where to find it.

2 Users, roles & permissions

Control who's on the team and exactly what each person can see and do.

Users add and manage team members. Roles & Permissions define what each role can access — from full admins to reps who only see their own book — with granular control over records and actions. Departments organize people into groups, and Activity & Logs is the audit trail of who did what across the system.

Best practice
Give reps the narrowest role that lets them do their job. You can always widen it — and the audit log means nothing happens off the record.

3 Pipelines, stages & automations

Define how deals move across the board and what the system does for you at each step.

Pipelines & Stages set the columns a deal moves through (from unqualified lead to closed) and can be required when a company is created, so every new seller starts on the board. Automations fire actions automatically — notifications, task creation, and the like — when something happens, so routine follow-through doesn't depend on memory.

4 Data tools

Everything that keeps your book clean, complete, and safe lives under the Data group.

Import your book from any CRM; enrich it from Google and AI (operating status, ratings, photos, logos, cuisine/type/price); clean & standardize, find duplicates, and link by email domain; check Record Counts; export to a spreadsheet; and take a full Data Backup. There's a dedicated guide for this whole workflow — see the Data help & user guide.

5 Paperwork engine

The templates and rules behind every document the platform generates.

Agreement Templates are the reusable, tokenized clauses and documents your listing agreements, NDAs, and ETRAs are built from. The LOI Builder Setup configures the letter-of-intent generator, and Valuation Rules set the factors and defaults the BOV / valuation engine uses. Get these right once and every generated document stays consistent and on-brand.

6 Branding & white-label

Make the platform yours, end to end.

Branding & Appearance sets your logo, firm name, and colors across the app, generated documents, data rooms, and outbound email. In General Settings you can also capture the brokerage's legal name and contact details used on agreements and BOVs, and even rename in-app labels (for example, what "Concept" is called) to match how your firm talks.

7 Integrations & keys

The connections that power enrichment, email, and more.

Under Integrations you connect Google Maps (the key behind location enrichment and photos), Gmail / SMTP (so mail sends and syncs on contacts and rooms), and manage API keys. If a tool ever says "add a key," this is where it goes.

Keys stay server-side
API keys are stored on the server and never exposed to buyers or in the browser. Add them once and the relevant tools light up.

8 AI settings & usage

Tune the assistant and keep an eye on what it costs.

AI Prompts and AI Engine control how the assistant behaves across the app (concept discovery, enrichment, auto-filing, and more). AI Usage shows exactly what's been run, how long it took, and estimated cost — filterable by time range — so AI stays a tool you control, not a black box.

9 Forms & firm-wide documents

The questionnaires you run and the documents the whole team shares.

Call Questionnaires build the structured seller-screening and interview forms your reps use on calls. Firm-wide documents — blank agreements, NDAs, onboarding decks — are published once and appear for every user; you manage them in the Documents library under the Firm-wide filter, so there's a single source of truth for the paperwork everyone needs.

10 FAQ

A rep can see things they shouldn't.

Check their role under Roles & Permissions and tighten what that role can access. Roles apply everywhere, so one change covers the whole app.

Where do I set the Google or Gmail keys?

Settings → Integrations. Google Maps powers location enrichment and photos; Gmail / SMTP powers email on contacts and data rooms.

How do I change the firm's logo and name everywhere?

Settings → Branding & Appearance for the logo and colors; General Settings for the legal name and contact details used on documents.

How do I publish a blank form for the whole team?

Open the Documents library, upload the file, and tick "Firm-wide" — it then appears for every user under the Firm-wide filter.
