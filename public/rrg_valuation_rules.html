Valuation Rules

The thresholds and disclosure language the AI applies to every BOV. These decide whether a deal is concluded on SDE or Adjusted EBITDA, when it drops to an asset sale, and what the report says when trailing financials are missing.
Command›Valuation Rules

BOV Valuation Basis

Deals with trailing sales below this value are concluded on SDE; at or above it, on Adjusted EBITDA. This is the single line that separates an owner-operator business from a managed one, so set it to where your buyer pool actually changes.
Applies to the next BOV built. Existing reports are not recalculated.

No-TTM Notice

Shown on a BOV when no trailing-twelve-month statement was provided and we are past Q1, so the valuation fell back to the previous fiscal year. Keep it plain — this is the line a buyer's accountant reads first.

Asset-Sale Floor

When trailing SDE is at or below this, the business has no going-concern value and is treated and marketed as an asset sale — equipment, leasehold, and licenses, not cash flow.
