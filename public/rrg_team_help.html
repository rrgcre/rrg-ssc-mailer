Team & Employee Records — Help & User Guide

Every team member has an employee record: their book of business and production at a glance, their contact and HR details, and — for administrators — payroll and tax. It's the fastest way to see what a rep is carrying, what they've closed, and what commission is still owed.

Team rosterProduction & commissionEmployee records

❖ Overview

Open Team from the left toolbar. Administrators land on the roster of everyone on the team; a rep goes straight to their own record.

An employee record reads like a contact record, but for a team member: a header with their photo and role, a row of production and commission KPIs, editable employee details, admin-only payroll & tax, and their Listings, Deals, and Agreements — each linking back to the live record.

📋 The Team roster

One line per team member, ranked by commission, with the numbers that matter across the whole firm.

The roster totals the team's active listings, deals, commission, received, waiting-on and pipeline at the top, then lists each member with the same figures. Click any name to open their record. Sort by any column to see your top producers, who's carrying the most listings, or who has the most commission still outstanding.

Who sees what
The roster with everyone's commission is admin-only. A rep who opens Team is taken to their own record; they never see peers' commission or payroll.

📊 KPIs explained

Every figure on the record is computed live from the rep's listings and deals — nothing is typed in by hand.

Active Listings
Open listings the rep owns (not closed or lost).
Deals
Listings that have a transaction — shown as open vs. closed.
Commission (GCI)
Gross Commission Income on closed deals — the firm's total commission before any split or referral.
Received
Commission actually paid to date across the rep's deals.
Waiting on
Commission owed but not yet received on closed deals — e.g. the second-half of a lease commission due at occupancy.
Net
The rep's share after the firm's agent split (GCI × agent %).
Pipeline
Total value of the rep's active listings — what's in flight.

🪪 Employee details

Contact and HR basics on the record, kept in one place.

Home address, personal email and phone, LinkedIn, start and end dates, emergency contact, and internal HR notes. A rep can update their own contact details; start date, end date and HR notes are administrator-only. The start date also shows in the record header.

🔒 Payroll & tax

Sensitive pay and tax data, visible only to administrators.

The payroll card captures payment method, W-9 on file (with the date received), tax classification, the tax ID used for 1099 reporting, and payment notes. This section is never shown to the employee or to peers — only administrators can see or edit it.

Handling sensitive IDs
Full SSNs are best not kept in plain text. If your data policy requires it, the record can store only the last four of the SSN plus the "W-9 on file" flag instead of the full number — ask an administrator to switch it to masked storage.

💰 Commission structure & split

The Net figure and every projected commission come from your firm's commission setup.

Under Settings → Accounting → Commission Structure an administrator configures the two fee methods (the tiered Modified Lehman rate card and a flat rate), the per-location minimum fee, and the firm-wide agent split — the share of GCI a rep keeps. The agent split drives the Net column on the roster and each record. The same rate card powers the Commission Forecaster and the projected GCI on every agreement, so the numbers always agree.

❓ FAQ

A rep's listing or deal isn't showing on their record.

The record collects listings and deals by owner. Make sure the listing's owner is set to that team member; ownership is matched by their name or username.

Why is "Waiting on" zero when a deal closed?

Waiting-on is the difference between commission due and commission paid on closed deals. It's zero once the full commission is marked paid — set the commission paid amount on the deal as installments come in.

Can reps see each other's commission?

No. The roster and payroll are admin-only; a rep only sees their own record.

Where does the agent split come from?

Settings → Accounting → Commission Structure, as a firm-wide percentage (default 50%). Change it there and every Net figure updates.
