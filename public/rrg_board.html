Drag to advance a listingMove a card across the pipeline and its stage updates instantly — no forms. The board is the deal, live.

Every stage opens its toolClick a stage on a card to jump straight into that step — Seller Call, Questionnaire, BOV, Data Room, Marketing Pack, Attack Plan, Closing.

Switch pipelinesRun separate boards for business sales and tenant rep — pick the pipeline from the selector and the board reflows to its stages.

Board or list viewToggle between the kanban board and a sortable, filterable list of the same listings — whichever way you work.
🔎
Filter & save your viewsSlice by owner, market, status, value or commission, and save the views you run most.

Start a listing in one stepCreate a listing with a business, market and client — it builds the data room and links the client automatically.

Value, days & commission on the cardEach card shows its valuation, days on market and commission at a glance — configurable in the card settings.

Buyer leads auto-attachBizBuySell and CoStar inquiries land on the matching listing as deduped buyers with a follow-up task already waiting.
🎯
Matching buyers on every listingOpen a listing and see the active buyers whose Buy-Box fits it — with one-click open and email.
🔒
Data room & documents built inEvery listing carries its confidential data room, agreements, NDAs, tours and files — all on the listing record.

Code name for confidential deals NewGive a blind listing a code name (“Project Blue Agave”) — it headlines the teaser and CIM so buyers and your team have a handle, while the real business name stays locked until NDA.
