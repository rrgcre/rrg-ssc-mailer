Agreement Builder — Help & User Guide

Turn any PDF into a signable agreement, send it for signature, and track it all the way to close. Place fields right on the document, let the rep fill every detail so the signer only signs, then capture the commission, renew before it lapses, and keep a court-ready audit trail — end to end, in one place.

🧭 Overview

Templates are managed under Settings → Documents → Agreement Templates (admin). Agreements themselves are created and sent from a contact, company, or deal.

The idea is simple: store each standard agreement once as a template, place the signature and data fields on it a single time, and reuse it forever. When it's time to send, the rep completes every detail — names, dates, terms, dollar amounts — so the person on the other end only has to sign. The three stages of the tool are Build the document, Send & sign it, and Close the loop — commission, renewals, and a signed audit trail.

🛠️ Build the document

Upload the signable PDF, then drop fields right onto it in the field designer. No re-typing later — the layout is set once and reused every time you send.

Create a template with + New template, give it a name and (optionally) a type, and upload the PDF you sign today. Open Design fields to place fields directly on the document:

  • Signature and initials — assigned to a specific signer.
  • Date, text, number, currency, email, and checkbox fields — each can be typed by a signer or auto-filled from the record (client name/email, your name, the brokerage legal name, today's date).
  • Every field is tied to a signer and can be marked required. Text starts at the top of the box and wraps, so long values stay readable.
Sensible defaults
New templates open with two signers already labeled Client and your firm's name, so you're not renaming the parties on every template.

AI auto-place New

Instead of placing every field by hand, click ✨ Auto-place fields after you upload. The assistant reads the document's text and geometry and proposes where the signature, date, and party fields belong — each shown with a gold outline for you to accept, nudge, or delete. It's the biggest time-saver in the builder; it runs an AI pass, which is a metered action.

Field groups & completeness check New

Drop a whole signature block — signature, printed name, date, and title — in one click instead of placing four fields separately. Before you save, the completeness check reviews the template and flags anything that would trip up a live send: a signer with no signature field, unlabeled fields, or a required field no one can fill. A badge tells you it's airtight before it ever goes out.

Dates, term & expiration

On the agreement you set the effective date and expiration. Use Effective = today, pick a term (1 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 years auto-fills the expiration), or choose Effective on full execution so the clock starts the moment the last party signs. Effective and expiration dates now show in the Documents list and on the agreement so nothing lapses unseen.

✍️ Send & sign

The rep fills everything in first; the signer only signs. Send by email or hand over a secure link.

When you send, you get a Complete the details step that lists every field you're responsible for — names, dates, dollar amounts, checkboxes. Auto-fill fields are already populated from the record, so you're just confirming. The signer opens a clean document, sees the values and checkbox states you set, and adds only their signature.

Sequential multi-signer: the client signs first and the broker countersigns last, in order. Each signer gets their own link; send by email straight from the record or copy the secure link to share yourself. You can watch each signer move from Sent to Viewed to Signed.

Locked after signing New

Security is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the screen. The details the rep fills are locked when the agreement is sent, and once the first signature is on the document the whole agreement is frozen — a later signer cannot change fields, values, or terms. What the first party agreed to is exactly what the last party signs.

💰 Close the loop

A signed agreement should do more than sit in a folder. The builder projects the commission to the deal, protects the renewal, and files a court-ready audit trail.

Commission & projected GCI New

On each agreement you pick the commission method for that deal:

  • Modified Lehman — the firm's tiered / marginal schedule. Each tranche of the transaction value earns its own tier's rate, summed for the gross commission.
  • Flat rate — a single percentage of the transaction value.
  • Flat fee — a fixed dollar amount negotiated for that deal.

Both firm methods (the Lehman rate card and the flat rate) are configured once by an admin under Settings → Accounting → Commission Structure; on the deal you just choose which one applies. The projected GCI calculates live as you enter the deal value (or pulls it from the linked deal), honoring the per-location minimum fee for multi-unit transactions. When the agreement is executed, the gross GCI flows to the linked deal's commission fields and a follow-up task opens for the owner to invoice and track it to payment.

What "GCI" means here
Gross Commission Income — the firm's total commission before any agent / house split or referral. The same Modified Lehman card powers the standalone Commission Forecaster, so the two always agree.

Renewal & expiration engine New

Nothing lapses by accident. The system watches every active agreement's expiration and creates a follow-up task for the owner as it approaches — at reminder thresholds an admin sets under the Renewal reminders card (default 60 and 30 days; the closer one is High priority). When it's time, Renew / extend clones the agreement into a fresh term in one click — new dates, same fields and document — as a draft you review and send. Once renewed, the original is marked so you can't double-renew it.

Audit certificate New

Every executed agreement gets a DocuSign-style Certificate of Completion appended to the final PDF: a unique document ID, and for each signer the Sent, Viewed, and Signed timestamps with IP addresses, plus an ESIGN Act / UETA statement. It's the court-ready trail that proves who signed what, when, and from where.

🗂️ Template library

Your standard agreements, stored once and reused — consistent every time, editable in one place.

The library lists every template with its type, document status, and when it was last used. Each template carries its own signer count and labels, the email subject and message that goes out with it, an optional greeting, and an on-execution automation that can fire when the agreement is fully signed. Mark a template active or inactive to control what reps can send. Print or edit any template from the row; delete removes the template and its document without touching agreements already created from it.

❓ FAQ

The broker name didn't auto-fill for the signer.

Auto-fill fields pull from the record and from your account. Make sure the field's source is set (e.g. "Me — name", "Brokerage — legal name") in the field designer, and that your profile and the firm's brokerage legal name are filled in. Auto-fill fields are completed for the signer and don't appear as blanks for them to fill.

Can a later signer change what an earlier one agreed to?

No. Once the first signature is on the document, the agreement is frozen server-side — fields, values, and terms are locked for every subsequent signer.

Which commission method should I pick?

Use Modified Lehman for standard tiered deals, Flat rate when the firm quotes one percentage, or Flat fee for a negotiated fixed amount. The two firm methods are set once in Settings → Accounting → Commission Structure; you just choose per deal.

Where does the projected commission go?

On execution the gross GCI writes to the linked deal's commission fields and opens a tracking task for the owner. If you leave the deal value blank on the agreement, it's pulled from the linked deal at execution.

How do renewal reminders get created?

Automatically. Any active agreement within the admin-set thresholds (default 60 / 30 days) of its expiration gets a follow-up task for the owner. Use Renew / extend on the agreement to spin up the next term.

Do I have to place every field by hand?

No — upload the PDF and click ✨ Auto-place fields to have the assistant propose them, then adjust. You can also drop a full signature block in one click.
