Subscribers

Your marketing audience — the people who get mass emails — kept in its own list, separate from your CRM contacts. Add subscribers by hand, paste a list, or pull them in from your contacts. Each subscriber controls their own preferences (all emails, only certain areas, or unsubscribed), and every mass email honors it.
