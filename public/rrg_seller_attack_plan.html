The Process

Confidential Marketing Sequence

A controlled, phased release — information goes out only as buyers qualify and commit. This protects the business and builds competitive tension.
Blind teaser
A no-name, one-page profile goes to the qualified target list — enough to attract, nothing that identifies the business.
NDA execution
Interested parties sign RRG's NDA and provide background and proof of financial capacity before anything else is released.
CIM release
Qualified, NDA-bound buyers receive the full Confidential Information Memorandum and overview package.
Management meetings & site visits
Vetted buyers meet ownership and tour discreetly. Buyer Q&A managed through RRG.
Call for offers → LOIs
A defined deadline drives written offers in parallel — creating the competitive tension that moves price and terms.
Best-and-final → selection
Shortlist buyers to best-and-final; select on price, certainty, and fit; execute LOI and move to PSA and diligence.

Confidentiality

Selling Without Tipping the Market

Restaurants and bars are fragile to a leaked sale — staff, vendors, landlords, and competitors. The whole process is built to keep it quiet until a deal is real.
Leverage

Creating Competitive Tension

The whole game on the sell side: never negotiate with one buyer. A defined process with a deadline and multiple qualified bidders is what moves price toward — and past — the top of the BOV range.
